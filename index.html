The Real Problem

Building Faster Isn't the Same as Building the Right Thing.

AI has made it easier than ever to turn an idea into working software. That's the exciting part. Then people start using it.

Someone wants a new feature. Someone else can't figure out the onboarding. A beta tester flags something weird. A customer sends an email. Your backlog grows.

"Not every request deserves a feature. Not every bug deserves the same urgency. And your loudest user isn't necessarily your most important signal."

Here's a distinction worth making:

Software Testing

Does the code work? QA, automated tests, bug reports. Catches what's broken before it ships. Your developers handle this.

User Feedback & Market Validation

Does it solve the right problem for the right people? This is about knowing what's worth building — and what isn't. This is where CM Launch Lab comes in.

Meanwhile, your competitors keep shipping — and you still need this thing to make money. So what actually deserves your attention next?

What I Do

You Don't Need More Feedback. You Need to Know What to Do With It.

You may already have more customer insight than you realize. It's sitting in support emails, conversations, reviews, feature requests, your backlog, analytics — and probably a few places you've forgotten about.

I help bring those signals together and turn them into something useful: hands-on product testing, Voice of Customer research, customer feedback analysis, backlog review, and facilitated prioritization.

You bring the vision and knowledge of your business. I bring the Product Owner lens, the questions, the testing, the customer perspective, and the structure to help you decide what deserves your limited time and money next.
